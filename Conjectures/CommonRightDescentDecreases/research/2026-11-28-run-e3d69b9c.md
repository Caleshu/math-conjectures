## Definitions

- **Length and $\mu$**: $\ell(x)$ is the inversion number of $x$. For odd positive $d=\ell(w)-\ell(x)$, $\mu(x,w)=[q^{(d-1)/2}]P_{x,w}(q)$, where $P_{x,w}$ is the Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomial. A common right descent lowers both endpoint lengths by one, preserving $d$.
- **Uncompressible pair**: There is no position $i$ with $x(i)=w(i)=j$ and $\#\{k<i:w(k)\geq j\}=\#\{k<i:x(k)\geq j\}$. Such a position could be deleted from both permutations, followed by relabeling.
- **Transposition graph**: Take any saturated Bruhat chain from $x$ to $w$. Its graph has the permutation positions as vertices and the position transpositions labeling the chain steps as edges. Its connected components are independent of the chain.

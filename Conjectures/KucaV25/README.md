# Kuca's conjecture, case n = 5: even terms of V(2,5)

**Status:** **Proved** (computer-assisted): the even terms of V(2,5) are exactly 2, 10 and 52. A Lean formalization is in progress: `Data.lean` builds, and the main proof (`Main.lean`, theorem `even_terms`) **will
be added once its kernel check finishes**. This case was also proved independently in a parallel session (see
[`../Handoff2026-11`](../Handoff2026-11/)), which went on to do many more V(a,b) and U(a,b) cases.

## Statement

V(2,n) starts 2, n. Each later term is the least integer larger than the previous one that has exactly one
representation as a sum of two earlier terms (not necessarily distinct).

Source: Kuca, *Additive bases and the Ulam-type sequences*, Acta Arith. 186 (2018); arXiv:1804.09594. The conjecture:
when n − 1 is a power of 2, the even terms of V(2,n) are exactly 2, 2n and 2n² + 2.

## Proof (n = 5)

1. Generate V(2,5) by the rule up to 5650 (`computations/gen.c`).
2. Assume the even terms are E = {2, 10, 52}. Then an odd u > 52 is a term iff exactly one of u − 2, u − 10, u − 52 is a
   term. That is a shift register on the odd terms, which is periodic with **period 694 from 2131** (`brent.c`,
   `per2.c`).
3. Even terms beyond 52 do not appear: every even m ≥ 5650 has at least two representations. Each even residue class
   mod 694 has at least 34 periodic witness pairs; the Lean file stores two per class (347 pairs). Even m < 5650 are
   checked directly.
4. Strong induction on m closes the argument (`verify2.c`).

For n = 9 the same shift register has period 803,114,720, so this residue method is too slow there. The parallel
session's prover did handle V(2,9).

  `wit`.
- `Main.lean` (pending): `IsV25 S` is the defining rule as a predicate. `R` is the explicit table-plus-period predicate.
  - The finite checks are `checkSmall`, `checkPer`, `checkEven`, `checkOdd` and `checkWit`, each by `decide +kernel`.
  - `agree` shows S = R by strong induction.
  - `even_terms : IsV25 S → ((S m ∧ m % 2 = 0) ↔ m = 2 ∨ m = 10 ∨ m = 52)`.

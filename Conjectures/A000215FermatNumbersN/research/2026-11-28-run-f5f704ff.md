**Target:** Lemma: for every odd prime $p$, $(4p)^4-1\nmid 2^{4p}-1$

2. Take a prime $q\mid 4p-1$. Then $q$ is odd and $q\ne p$, and $d=\mathrm{ord}_q(2)$ divides both $4p$ and $q-1$.

8. Hence the lemma holds.

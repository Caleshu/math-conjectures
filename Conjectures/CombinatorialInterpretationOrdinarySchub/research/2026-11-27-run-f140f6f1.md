## Original conjecture

Finding a combinatorial interpretation of these numbers is a central open problem in Schubert calculus.

**Normalized statement.** Give an explicit family of finite combinatorial sets $\mathcal S(u,v,w)$ such that for every triple of finite-support permutations $u,v,w\in S_\infty$, $|\mathcal S(u,v,w)|=c_{u,v}^w$, where $\mathfrak S_u\mathfrak S_v=\sum_z c_{u,v}^z\mathfrak S_z$.

## Source

## Definitions

- **Schubert polynomial**: $\mathfrak S_u$ is the polynomial representative indexed by $u$ of a Schubert cohomology class. The ordinary structure constants are the coefficients of products in this basis.
- **Geometric interpretation**: For degree-compatible $u,v,w\in S_n$, the coefficient counts flags satisfying the Schubert conditions indexed by $u,v,w_0w$ relative to three general flags, where $w_0$ is the longest permutation.

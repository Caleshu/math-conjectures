## Definitions

- **$G(x,w)$**: For a connected transposition graph on $m$ positions, $G(x,w)=(d-m-c+2)/2$, where $d$ is the difference of the endpoint inversion numbers and $c$ counts all cycles of the relative permutation.
- **Uncompressible type-A interval**: The endpoints are permutations in $S_m$. No position $i$ has a common value $j=x(i)=w(i)$ with equal counts of entries at least $j$ before $i$ in the two permutations.
- **Connected transposition graph**: The position transpositions along a saturated Bruhat chain from $x$ to $w$ form the edges of a connected graph on $\{1,\ldots,m\}$. Connectedness is independent of the chain.
- **Top permitted coefficient**: For odd $d>0$, the degree bound for the Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomial permits degree at most $(d-1)/2$. The specified coefficient is zero if the actual degree is smaller.

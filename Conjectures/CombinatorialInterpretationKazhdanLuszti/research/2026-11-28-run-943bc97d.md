## Definitions

- **$[q^k]P_{x,w}(q)$**: The coefficient of $q^k$ in the Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomial of the Bruhat interval $[x,w]$. The normalization has $P_{w,w}=1$ and $\deg P_{x,w}\leq(\ell(w)-\ell(x)-1)/2$ for $x<w$.
- **Bruhat interval**: $[x,w]=\{z\in W:x\leq z\leq w\}$ in Bruhat order. In $S_n$, the length $\ell$ counts inversions.

### [P-949300] Fermionic p-adic integral plus an Euler-factor identity

By agent:strategist. Status **review_needs_work**, level written proof attempt.

**Target:** Disproof of the full conjecture (k=27, and every k with an odd prime cube factor). Proof for odd p^e with e<=2.

1. For odd $p$, $\mu_{-1}(a+p^N\mathbb Z_p)=(-1)^a$ is a bounded measure, and $E_m=\int(2y+1)^m d\mu_{-1}$ (C1).
2. The non-units $2y+1\in p\mathbb Z_p$ form the ball $y\equiv(p-1)/2 \pmod p$. Substituting $y=(p-1)/2+pz$ gives $(-1)^{(p-1)/2}p^mE_m$. So $(1-\varepsilon_pp^m)E_m=E^*_m$, the integral over units.
3. On units, $u^{\varphi(p^e)}\equiv1 \pmod{p^e}$, so $E^*$ is purely $\varphi(p^e)$-periodic mod $p^e$.
4. Hence $E_{m+\varphi}-E_m\equiv-\varepsilon_pp^mE_m \pmod{p^e}$.
5. Case $e\le2$, $m\ge2$: the right side vanishes, which gives periodicity for $n\ge1$.
6. Case $e\ge3$, $m=2$: the right side is $\varepsilon_pp^2\not\equiv0$, so $a(1+\varphi)\not\equiv a(1)$.
7. Pure periodicity with any $P$ together with eventual periodicity with period $\varphi$ forces pure periodicity with period $\varphi$ (shift by multiples of $P$). That is a contradiction.
8. Smallest instance: $k=27$, with $a(10)\equiv17$ and $a(19)\equiv10$, while $a(1)=1$.

## Failed approaches

- **Using the Kummer congruence $E_{m+\varphi(p^e)}\equiv E_m$ directly for all $m\ge2$** (strategist): The congruence holds only for $m\ge e$, or more precisely when $m+v_p(E_m)\ge e$. It fails at $m=2$ when $e\ge3$, which is exactly why the conjecture fails.
- **Extending the fermionic-measure proof to p=2** (strategist): On $\mathbb Z_2$, $(-1)^a$ is not a distribution: $(-1)^a+(-1)^{a+2^N}=0\ne(-1)^a$. The 2-part needs Stern's theorem or a separate argument.
- **Look for a counterexample at a modulus $p^2$ or at a power of 2** (construction): For $p^2$ the needed condition $m\ge e$ already holds at $m=2$, so the sequence is purely periodic from $n=1$. Mod 8 and mod 16 I checked purity by hand (periods 4 and 8), consistent with Stern.

## Original conjecture

Stanley's Problem~10 asks for a combinatorial interpretation of these multiplicities.

**Normalized statement.** Give an explicit family of finite combinatorial sets $\mathcal K(\lambda,\mu,\nu)$ such that for every integer $n\geq1$ and every $\lambda,\mu,\nu\vdash n$, $|\mathcal K(\lambda,\mu,\nu)|=g_{\lambda,\mu,\nu}$.

## Definitions

- **Kronecker coefficient**: A partition $\lambda\vdash n$ indexes an irreducible complex representation $V^\lambda$ of $S_n$. The integer $g_{\lambda,\mu,\nu}$ is the multiplicity of $V^\nu$ in $V^\lambda\otimes V^\mu$.
